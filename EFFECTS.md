# PRISM motion effects

## Scroll showreel reveal

Reference language: Apple-style pinned scroll section where a fullscreen video is scrubbed by scroll, scales down, and lands inside a product/device frame.

For PRISM:

- The first screen can start as a fullscreen showreel.
- During scroll, the showreel scales down and moves into a montage monitor.
- The final lockup shows an editing station: monitor frame, clip thumbnails, timeline tracks, and a playhead.
- Technical terms: `scroll-scrubbed video`, `pinned section`, `masked video reveal`, `monitor mockup reveal`, `timeline UI reveal`.
