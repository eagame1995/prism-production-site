# PRISM motion effects

## Scroll showreel reveal

Reference language: Apple-style pinned scroll section where a fullscreen video is scrubbed by scroll, scales down, and lands inside a product/device frame.

For PRISM:

- The first screen can start as a fullscreen showreel.
- During scroll, the showreel scales down and moves into a brand frame.
- The final lockup can be a prism, camera monitor, edit timeline, or hero frame.
- Technical terms: `scroll-scrubbed video`, `pinned section`, `masked video reveal`, `device mockup reveal`.
